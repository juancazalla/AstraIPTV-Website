PRIVACY · IPHONE AND IPAD

Your privacy.

How Astra IPTV uses local storage, iCloud and the connections needed to play your sources.

Last updated: October 9, 2026. Applies to Astra IPTV for iPhone and iPad (com.astra.iptv).

Who develops Astra

Astra IPTV is developed by Juan Cazalla Estrella. For app help or privacy questions, email juan.cazalla.17@gmail.com. You can also open a public issue. Do not post passwords, private playlists or personal information.

You do not create an Astra account. Credentials you enter belong to your content providers; your iCloud account belongs to Apple.

Data on your device

The app stores your playlists, catalog, favorites, history, playback progress, category organization and preferences to provide your library and resume playback. Connection details are stored in the system keychain. Playlist and content URLs may also contain credentials; do not share them.

Images and program guide data are cached. Local technical logs may contain player error codes. Astra has no advertising network or analytics SDK of its own and does not request the device advertising identifier. Trailers open outside Astra in YouTube or your browser, where Google’s privacy and advertising policies apply.

Private iCloud synchronization

When iCloud is available, the app syncs playlists, connection details, category organization, favorites, history, progress and settings to the private CloudKit database in your Apple account. This includes the URLs and credentials needed to download compatible sources again on another device. Each device downloads its own catalog. Locally imported M3U files must be imported again on another device; these files and video files are not uploaded.

Sync runs when you open the app, during use, when leaving if the system permits, and when requested in Settings. Demo mode does not sync. The developer has no dashboard for viewing the contents of your private database. Apple processes and stores this data under its iCloud terms and privacy policy.

Your sources and external services

To import playlists, load a guide or play content, Astra connects directly to the addresses you add. Servers receive requests, your IP address and any authentication details needed. Providers and image servers apply their own policies.

TMDB may receive a movie or series title and year, a content identifier, or cast and director names to complete information and images. MDBList may receive a content identifier to retrieve ratings when that integration is configured. These services receive the network requests necessary to answer those queries, including your IP address. Images hosted by third parties may also load, including the demo’s Unsplash images. Astra does not send your provider credentials to these services as part of metadata queries.

In the App Store version 1.0.0, tapping “Watch trailer” opens a validated YouTube link in the YouTube app or your browser. Astra does not embed or load the YouTube player. Earlier TestFlight builds may still use the embedded player; YouTube may collect device, playback and security information there.

Read the policies of TMDB, MDBList, Google/YouTube and Unsplash. Apple may manage system diagnostics according to your device privacy settings.

Retention and deletion

Your library is retained while you use the app and keep your playlists. You can delete a playlist in Settings. Astra removes its catalog, favorites, history and active credentials and syncs the deletion when iCloud is available. Technical deletion markers remain to prevent an offline device from restoring old data.

Manage iCloud data in your Apple account settings. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee deletion of iCloud data or persistent keychain entries. If you stop using Astra, delete your playlists first, allow the deletion to sync to your devices, and review your iCloud storage. Use the contact above for privacy questions. The developer cannot directly delete your private iCloud database.

If you contact support

Your email includes your address and any information you choose to share. It is used to respond to your inquiry. GitHub issues are public; do not include private information. Your email service and GitHub process these messages under their own terms.

This website and policy updates

This website includes no analytics, advertising, forms or first-party cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical visitor information under the GitHub privacy statement. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
