BEFORE YOU START

Good to know.

Can I download it yet?

Not yet. The iOS, iPadOS and tvOS version is in development and is not available on the App Store. This website introduces the project and its features.

Does it include channels or subscriptions?

No. Astra IPTV does not sell or provide channels, playlists, movies, series or TV subscriptions. You must add your own sources and have the necessary rights or permission to play them.

What happens to my data?

The current version stores the library and preferences on the device, with credentials in the system keychain. It includes no advertising or analytics. Sources and external services you use receive the requests needed to provide their features.

Does this website use cookies or tracking?

This website includes no analytics, advertising, forms or first-party cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log technical visitor data under its privacy statement.
